# The customers who grow your business are invisible. We built Ubu-Tangaza to change that.

*By the Ubu-Tangaza team — ubutangaza.biz*

Walk into any good coffee shop, salon, or boutique in Nairobi and ask the owner where their customers come from. You'll get the same answer almost every time: *"People tell their friends."*

Then ask a harder question: **which people?**

Silence. Nobody knows. The regular who has quietly sent fifteen friends through the door gets the same handshake as everyone else. The customer who posts about the shop every week earns nothing for it. Word of mouth is the engine of every small business — and it's the one channel no one can see, measure, or reward.

That's the problem Ubu-Tangaza exists to solve.

## What it is

Ubu-Tangaza is a rewards platform for the businesses that grow by word of mouth. It does three things:

**1. It makes advocacy visible.** Your customers get a personal shareable link. When they refer a friend, post about you, or bring people to your event, it's captured — with **proof**. A photo of the receipt. The actual post. The person who showed up. You're never guessing who did what; you can see it and track it, activity by activity.

**2. It lets you reward your way.** You decide what an action is worth and what it earns — cash, airtime, a voucher, a discount, free product, merch. Any amount, any currency, any form. A referral can be worth more than a repost. Five referrals can unlock something bigger than one. You set the levels; your community climbs them.

**3. It makes the whole thing trustworthy.** Every approved activity is recorded on-chain — who did what, vouched for by the business, tamper-proof. Not because your customers care about blockchains (they never see one — they sign in with Google), but because a reward program is a promise, and promises are worth more when nobody can quietly rewrite them. Your reward budget is written once and can never be raised behind your community's back. Going viral can never blow your costs.

## The part that matters: zero babysitting

Every loyalty tool that failed a small business failed the same way — it became a second job. Another dashboard, another queue, another thing to check at closing time.

So we built Ubu-Tangaza to run itself. Set up your activities and rewards once — it takes minutes. Share one link. Proof arrives attached to every claim, so approving is a glance and a tap, and much of it verifies automatically. The result on your side is simple: a live view of who's actually growing your business, and what you owe them.

## For the people doing the talking

If you love a business, you're already doing the work — recommending it, posting it, dragging friends there. Ubu-Tangaza just makes sure it counts. Sign in with an account you already have. Nothing to install. Do what you already do, attach the proof, and watch your progress climb toward rewards the business actually honors. Real things — not points that expire into nothing.

## Where it comes from

Ubu-Tangaza was born at the Avalanche Team1 Game Jam in Nairobi, where it placed among the top projects — 155 builders joined a campaign in a single night, and the first businesses registered themselves through the product before the demo was over. It's now part of an accelerator program building for retail and SME loyalty, and we're onboarding our first pilot businesses: the coffee shops, salons, and boutiques Kenya actually runs on.

The name says the mission. *Ubuntu* — I am because we are. *Tangaza* — spread the word. A business and its community, growing each other, with receipts.
