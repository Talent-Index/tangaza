How it works

Four steps. Zero dashboard babysitting.

  1. Say what you rewardReferral, a post about you, a visit, an event — and what each is worth to you.
  2. Share one linkYour customers join in seconds. Each gets a personal link to bring friends with.
  3. Approve with one tapProof comes attached — the post, a photo, the referred name. Approve or reject from your phone.
  4. Honour the rewardCustomers see exactly where they stand ("2 more to a free cut"). You see exactly what you owe.
