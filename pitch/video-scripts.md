# Video scripts — Ubu-Tangaza

Two videos, both already built as self-playing motion graphics:

- `marketing-video.html` — 36s, evergreen, for X/Instagram/WhatsApp status and the website
- `launch-video.html` — 63s, the announcement, for the launch post and partner outreach

## Making the MP4 (5 minutes)

1. Open the HTML file in Chrome, press **F11** (fullscreen), then **⏵**.
2. Record: **OBS** (window capture, 1080p) · macOS **⇧⌘5** · or **Loom**.
3. Let it loop once fully, stop, trim the ends. Done.
4. Music: one warm afrobeat/amapiano instrumental under everything (30–40% volume);
   both videos read fully muted — captions are burned in — so music is flavor, not load-bearing.
5. Stronger version: intercut phone footage from your events (people signing up,
   the approval moment on stage) between scenes 3–5. The graphic carries the message;
   the faces carry the feeling.

## Where each video goes (distribution map)

- **Launch video:** pinned X post + LinkedIn from founders, WhatsApp broadcast to every
  event attendee, sent with every partner email (Avalanche, Africa's Talking, agencies).
- **Marketing video:** looping on the homepage, WhatsApp status of every team member and
  advocate (ask them — it's an engagement type!), org onboarding decks, counter screens
  at partner venues.
- Both: native upload everywhere (never a YouTube link on X), captions always on,
  first 3 seconds must show the brand — assume 80% watch muted and 50% stop by 0:10.
